CigMail

A fast, keyboard-first email client that brings all your accounts into one inbox.

CigMail is a desktop and Android email app for Gmail (through the Gmail API), Outlook / Microsoft 365 (through Microsoft Graph) and any IMAP/SMTP server. It keeps your mail in a local cache on your device, so reading and searching is instant and works offline.

Features

  • One unified inbox for all your accounts, with split views such as Important, News and Updates
  • Keyboard shortcuts for everything: archive, snooze, reply, search, undo
  • Full-text search, undo send, snooze, drafts and contact suggestions
  • Optional AI summaries and writing help, run only when you ask for them
  • Remote images and tracking pixels are blocked by default

How CigMail uses your Google account

When you connect a Gmail account, CigMail asks for permission to read, organize and send your email. It uses that permission only to show your mail in the app and to carry out what you do there, such as archiving, labeling, replying and sending. CigMail has no central server and no ads, and it never sells your data. See the privacy policy for details.
